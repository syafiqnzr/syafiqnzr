### About Me

I'm about a year into my career in **infrastructure**, working hands-on with Linux systems, monitoring, and the fundamentals of security. I'm not claiming to be an expert — I'm someone who enjoys understanding how systems actually run, break, and get fixed, and I'm deliberately steering my growth toward **DevOps and Security**.

- 🔧 Comfortable with day-to-day **Linux administration** — the command line is home turf
- 📊 Working knowledge of **monitoring** — keeping an eye on what's healthy and what's not
- 🔒 Building up **security fundamentals** — thinking about systems from a "how could this go wrong" angle
- 🌱 Currently learning: **automation & CI/CD, containers, and cloud basics**
- 🎯 Goal: become the engineer who keeps infrastructure both **reliable and secure**
